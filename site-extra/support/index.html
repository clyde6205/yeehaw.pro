Support

Common questions:

The app is slow or a page crashed

Open Apps → Memory Saver, close extra tabs, and make sure Android System WebView is updated in Google Play.

How do I use less data?

Turn on Data Saver in settings. On a weak signal the browser also switches to text-first automatically.

How do I erase my data?

In the app use "Forget me" in settings, or clear app storage in Android settings.
